Day by Day

Your Kovalam Itinerary

A curated 2-day plan to make the most of your Kovalam beach getaway.

Day 1 — Beach & Water Sports

  • Morning — Lighthouse Beach

    Begin your day at the iconic Lighthouse Beach. Catch the morning light on the golden sands and enjoy a fresh coconut by the shore.

  • Afternoon — Samudra Beach

    Head to the quieter Samudra Beach for a relaxed swim and beach activities away from the crowds.

  • Evening — Sunset at Hawah Beach

    End the day with a breathtaking Arabian Sea sunset at Hawah Beach — one of Kerala's most magical golden hours.

Day 2 — Culture & Adventure

  • Morning — Kovalam Art Gallery

    Explore the local art gallery to discover the region's rich cultural heritage through paintings, sculptures and crafts.

  • Afternoon — Water Sports

    Try parasailing, surfing or jet skiing at Lighthouse Beach with expert local instructors by your side.

  • Evening — Ayurvedic Massage

    Unwind with an authentic Kerala Ayurvedic massage at one of the beachside wellness spas — the perfect end to your trip.

Additional Activities

  • Promenade Stroll

    Walk the lively Beach Promenade lined with cafes, souvenir shops and local artisans — perfect for an evening out.

  • Explore Local Markets

    Browse the bustling local markets for handmade crafts, fresh spices and Kerala souvenirs to take home.

  • Lighthouse Climb

    Climb the Kovalam Lighthouse for a sweeping panoramic view of the three beaches and the shimmering Arabian Sea.
