Day by Day

Your Wayanad Itinerary

A curated 3-day plan to experience the best of Wayanad's forests, peaks, waterfalls and tribal heritage.

Day 1 — Peaks & Caves

  • Morning — Chembra Peak Trek

    Start early for the iconic Chembra Peak trek. The trail leads to a heart-shaped lake near the summit — one of the most romantic views in all of Kerala.

  • Afternoon — Edakkal Caves

    Explore these ancient caves with rare Neolithic pictorial writings carved into rock — a window into civilisations over 6,000 years old.

  • Evening — Lakkidi Viewpoint

    Watch the sun dip below the misty Ghats from Lakkidi, the gateway to Wayanad — the clouds roll in spectacularly at dusk.

Day 2 — Falls & Wildlife

  • Morning — Soochipara Falls

    Trek through dense shola forest to reach the three-tiered Soochipara waterfall — a magical cascade perfect for a refreshing swim.

  • Afternoon — Muthanga Jungle Safari

    Embark on a jeep safari through Muthanga Wildlife Reserve, home to wild elephants, tigers, leopards and over 200 species of birds.

  • Evening — Pookode Lake Boating

    End the day with a serene boat ride on Pookode Lake, a freshwater lake surrounded by evergreen forest at 770m elevation.

Day 3 — Plantations & Dam

  • Morning — Coffee Plantation Walk

    Walk through fragrant coffee and spice estates with a local guide — learn how Wayanad's legendary coffee is grown, harvested and processed.

  • Afternoon — Banasura Sagar Dam

    Visit India's largest earthen dam and take a boat ride across its vast reservoir dotted with small islands — stunning at any time of year.

  • Evening — Tribal Heritage Village

    Visit an Adivasi tribal village to experience traditional art, music, forest knowledge and the warm hospitality of Wayanad's indigenous communities.

Book jungle safari permits in advance — spots are limited and fill quickly during peak season.

Travel Tips

  • Best Time to Visit

    October to May for pleasant weather. Post-monsoon (Oct–Nov) is spectacular — waterfalls are at their fullest and the forests are emerald green.

  • Getting There

    Nearest airport: Calicut International (Kozhikode), 90km. Nearest railway: Kozhikode station with taxi connections to Wayanad.

  • Local Transport

    Renting a car or bike is strongly recommended — destinations are spread across the district and public transport is limited.

  • What to Pack

    Light layers for cool evenings, trekking shoes, insect repellent and a rain jacket — even in dry season, mist and drizzle are common.
