Top Destinations

Places to Discover
in Kerala

From misty tea gardens to sun-drenched beaches — each destination is a story waiting to be lived.

01 — Alappuzha

Alleppey

Famous for its tranquil backwaters and iconic houseboats, Alleppey is a must-visit for nature lovers seeking peaceful waterway journeys.

Houseboat · Nature

02 — Ernakulam

Kochi

A vibrant port city rich in culture, with historic architecture, Chinese fishing nets and a buzzing contemporary art scene.

Culture · History

03 — Idukki

Munnar

A scenic hill station draped in rolling tea plantations, misty mountains and cool air. Perfect for trekking and wildlife spotting.

Hills · Tea Gardens

04 — Thiruvananthapuram

Kovalam

One of Kerala's most famous crescent beaches, beloved for sunbathing, Ayurvedic therapies and Arabian Sea sunsets.

Beach · Wellness

05 — Thiruvananthapuram

Varkala

Known for dramatic red laterite cliffs, the sacred Papanasam Beach and a vibrant cliff-top café and yoga culture.

Beach · Spiritual

06 — Kozhikode

Kozhikode

Historic Calicut — famous for its beaches, Mananchira Square and legendary Malabar cuisine including the iconic Kozhikode biryani.

History · Food

07 — Thiruvananthapuram

Trivandrum

Kerala's capital blends ancient temples, colonial museums and Kovalam Beach. The magnificent Padmanabhaswamy Temple is unmissable.

Temples · Capital

08 — Wayanad

Wayanad

Mist-shrouded forests, ancient tribal caves, thundering waterfalls and sprawling coffee estates — Kerala's most untamed highland escape.

Forest · Wildlife

09 — Idukki

Thekkady

Home to Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary — Kerala's spice capital where cardamom, pepper and cinnamon perfume every trail.

Wildlife · Spice

10 — Thrissur

Thrissur

Kerala's cultural capital, home to the magnificent Thrissur Pooram — one of Asia's grandest temple festivals with caparisoned elephants.

Festivals · Culture

11 — Kannur

Kannur

The land of looms, lores and legends. Famous for Theyyam rituals, pristine beaches and the historic St. Angelo Fort on the coast.

Culture · Beach

12 — Kollam

Kollam

Gateway to Ashtamudi Lake — Kerala's second-largest backwater lake, famed for houseboat cruises, cashew processing and sunset vistas.

Backwaters · Lake

13 — Palakkad

Palakkad

The granary of Kerala — home to the ancient Palakkad Fort, the Silent Valley National Park and lush paddy fields stretching to the horizon.

Nature · History

14 — Malappuram

Malappuram

A land steeped in Malabar heritage, known for the Kottakkunnu hill park, Nilambur teak forests and the sacred Thirunavaya temple on the Bharathapuzha.

Heritage · Forest

15 — Kasaragod

Kasaragod

Kerala's northernmost district — home to the majestic Bekal Fort, pristine Kappil beach, the ancient Ananthapura Lake Temple and lush cashew groves stretching to the horizon.

Fort · Beach

16 — Kottayam

Kottayam

The land of letters, latex and lakes — sitting between Vembanad Lake and the Western Ghats, blessed with rubber plantations, historic churches and Kumarakom bird sanctuary.

Backwaters · Nature

17 — Pathanamthitta

Pathanamthitta

Known as the Pilgrim's Capital of Kerala — home to the Sabarimala temple, Konni elephant training centre and the scenic Pamba River winding through forested hills.

Pilgrimage · Nature

A grand vegetarian feast served on a banana leaf — 28 dishes including avial, olan, thoran, papad, pickles and payasam. Eaten with the right hand, seated on the floor. Onam's crowning glory.
