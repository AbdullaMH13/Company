Top Destinations

Places to Discover
in Kerala

From misty tea gardens to sun-drenched beaches — each destination is a story waiting to be lived.

01 — Backwaters

Alleppey

Famous for its tranquil backwaters and iconic houseboats, Alleppey is a must-visit for nature lovers and those seeking peaceful waterway journeys through lush greenery.

Houseboat · Nature

02 — Heritage City

Kochi

A vibrant port city rich in culture, with historic architecture, Chinese fishing nets, Fort Kochi's colonial charm and a buzzing contemporary art scene.

Culture · History

03 — Hill Station

Munnar

A scenic hill station draped in rolling tea plantations, misty mountains and cool air. Perfect for trekking, wildlife spotting and escaping the heat.

Hills · Tea Gardens

04 — Beach

Kovalam

One of Kerala's most famous crescent beaches, beloved for sunbathing, Ayurvedic therapies, water sports and spectacular Arabian Sea sunsets.

Beach · Wellness

05 — Cliffs & Coast

Varkala

Known for dramatic red laterite cliffs plunging into the sea, the sacred Papanasam Beach and a vibrant cliff-top café and yoga culture.

Beach · Spiritual

06 — Malabar City

Kozhikode

Historic Calicut — a city famous for its beaches, Mananchira Square and the legendary Malabar cuisine, including the iconic Kozhikode biryani.

History · Food

07 — Capital City

Trivandrum

Kerala's capital blends ancient temples, colonial museums and Kovalam Beach. The magnificent Padmanabhaswamy Temple alone makes it a pilgrim of culture.

Temples · Capital

10 — Highland District

Wayanad

Mist-shrouded forests, ancient tribal caves, thundering waterfalls and sprawling coffee estates — Kerala's most untamed highland escape.

Forest · Wildlife
