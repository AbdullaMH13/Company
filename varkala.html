Day by Day

Your Varkala Itinerary

A curated 2-day guide to experiencing the best of Varkala's cliffs, culture and coastline.

Day 1 — Beach & Temple

  • Morning — Varkala Beach

    Start your day on the pristine sands of Varkala Beach, surrounded by towering red cliffs and crystal-clear waves.

  • Afternoon — Janardhana Swamy Temple

    Visit the ancient temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu, known for its serene atmosphere and remarkable architecture.

  • Evening — Sunset at Papanasam Beach

    Witness a breathtaking sunset at the sacred Papanasam Beach — the golden sky against the cliffs is unforgettable.

Day 2 — Adventure & Culture

  • Morning — Paragliding

    Take a thrilling paragliding ride over Varkala's stunning coastline — the aerial view of the cliffs is spectacular.

  • Afternoon — Varkala Cliff

    Explore the famous Varkala Cliff promenade lined with cafés, yoga studios and boutique shops with ocean views.

  • Evening — Ayurvedic Treatment

    Unwind with an authentic Kerala Ayurvedic massage at one of the many clifftop wellness spas.

Additional Activities

  • Varkala Lighthouse

    Walk to the lighthouse for sweeping panoramic views of the Arabian Sea and the dramatic red cliff face.

  • Yoga & Meditation

    Join a sunrise yoga session at one of the many clifftop retreat studios — a truly spiritual experience.

  • Cliff Market Shopping

    Browse the vibrant cliff-side market for handmade jewellery, Kashmiri crafts and Kerala spices.
